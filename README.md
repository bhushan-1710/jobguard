# JobGuard AI

JobGuard AI is an AI-powered fake job posting detection platform designed to help job seekers identify fraudulent job listings before they apply. The platform leverages machine learning, natural language processing (NLP), and behavioral analysis techniques to analyze job postings, detect suspicious patterns, and provide an explainable fraud risk score. Our goal is to reduce online recruitment scams and create a safer, more transparent job search experience.
